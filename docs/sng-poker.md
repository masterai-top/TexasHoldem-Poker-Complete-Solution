# SNG Poker | Sit & Go Tournament

## Overview

SNG stands for Sit & Go.

Unlike scheduled tournaments, a Sit & Go tournament normally starts after the
required number of players has registered.

## Basic Workflow

```text
Waiting for Players
        ↓
Registration Complete
        ↓
Tournament Start
        ↓
Table Assignment
        ↓
Game
        ↓
Elimination
        ↓
Final Ranking
        ↓
Settlement
